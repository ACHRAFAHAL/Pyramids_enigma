## Project Overview

**Pyramids Enigma** is an interactive, educational web experience designed to guide users through the evolution, techniques, and social facets of pyramid construction in Ancient Egypt. From evolutionary designs to modern architectural inquiries—this journey invites both discovery and engagement.

👉 Live demo: [Pyramids Enigma](https://achrafahal.github.io/Pyramids_enigma/)

---

## Features

- **Evolution of Pyramids**  
  Trace the transformation from humble mastabas to Giza’s monumental giants, and witness the bold progression of engineering throughout time.

- **Engineering Hypotheses**  
  Explore key theories on how the Egyptians managed to lift and precisely place multi-ton limestone blocks—covering ramp systems, internal ramps, water lifts, and even early concrete use.

- **Builders’ Stories**  
  Discover the truth about the pyramid workforce: skilled, organized, and well-supported—far from the myths of slave labor. Learn about their city, logistics, medical care, and life.

- **Interactive “Pyramid Scholar” AI**  
  Submit your questions and receive enlightening responses powered by an AI scholar focused on ancient Egyptian pyramidology.

- **Primary Evidence Highlights**  
  Delve into Merer's logbook—a unique 4,500-year-old papyrus that meticulously records logistics, manpower, and rations during the Great Pyramid’s construction.
